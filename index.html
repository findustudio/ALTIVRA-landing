— Philosophy

Every app I tried either did one thing well, or six things badly. So I built the one I'd actually fly with.

Altivra began the way most pilot tools should begin — on a layover, in a hotel room, after a ten-hour duty. I was reconciling a duty roster from one app, a logbook from another, a fatigue tracker built in a spreadsheet, and a pay calculator I'd cobbled together over the years. Every tool knew its own job. None of them knew about each other.

The modern flight deck is a masterpiece of integration. The instruments talk. The automation understands intent. Yet the pilot's own life — our flights, our rest, our pay — lives in a patchwork of apps that haven't spoken since the sim session that introduced them.

Altivra is the refusal of that patchwork. One app. Six tools. Every module aware of every other. Your roster feeds your rest tracker. Your logbook feeds your pay verification. Your fatigue feeds your briefing. The flight deck stays uncluttered, because the deck you live with should be as elegant as the one you fly.

The system

One app. Six integrated tools.

Every module knows about every other. Your roster feeds your rest tracker. Your logbook feeds your pay. Your fatigue feeds your briefing. Finally in sync.

01 — BRIEFING

Briefing Room

Weather, route, and crew briefing for every sector — auto-pulled the night before. Walk to the plane already prepared.

02 — ROSTER

Roster

Smart roster import for supported airlines — new formats added as pilots send us theirs. Duty, rest, and block hours at a glance. Manual entry and CSV import work for every pilot in the meantime.

03 — REST

Rest & Fatigue

FRMS-aware tracking with a circadian-timed layover advisor. Reads sleep data from any source you already use — ring trackers, fitness wearables, Apple Watch, or manual entry. Know when you're safe to fly, and when your body needs to sleep.

04 — LOGBOOK

Digital Logbook

Every flight, every hour, every airport — visualized on a world map. Logbook migration from any major pilot app or spreadsheet format. PDF export that looks official. FlightAware enrichment included — matched to real routing, gates, and actual block times (fair-use quota).

05 — PAY

Pay Tracker

Keep your own record of pay, per diem and extras — with your block hours, duty days and sectors filled in automatically from your logbook. Pilots on supported contracts also get full payslip verification against their collective agreement, calculated on your iPad.

06 — INSIGHTS

Insights

Your career in numbers. Hours by aircraft, routes flown, airports visited, year-over-year growth. The story your logbook can't tell.

Frequently asked

Questions pilots ask first.

When does Altivra launch?
Altivra is in final testing. We're shipping when it's right, rather than to a date. Leave your email and you'll get one message the day the App Store link goes live.
Is my data private?
Your roster, logbook, fatigue, and pay data are yours. Stored encrypted in transit and at rest on secure cloud infrastructure so they sync across your devices. Never sold. Never shared with your airline. Export everything and delete your account at any time.
Who builds Altivra?
Altivra is built by Altivra Studios, an independent software studio based in Mexico. It comes out of firsthand experience with airline operations — the roster, the logbook, the fatigue rules and the pay rules are modelled on how the work actually happens, not on how it looks from outside.
Does it work offline?
Yes — Altivra is offline-first. Every module works at FL380, in the crew room, at a layover hotel on bad Wi-Fi. Changes sync automatically when you're back online. You never lose a logbook entry to spotty connectivity.
What iPad do I need?
Any iPad running iPadOS 26 or newer. We support the versions we actually test on — rather than claim wider support and let you find out the hard way. Altivra is built natively with SwiftUI: it feels fast on an iPad Mini and looks gorgeous on a 13" Pro. If your airline already approved your iPad as an EFB, you're good.
Will it import my airline's roster?
Altivra launches with full PDF roster auto-import for supported airlines, including online trip sheets. New formats are added as pilots send us their rosters — we are working through the major crew systems (Sabre, AIMS, Jeppesen), which unlock many airlines at once rather than one at a time. If your airline is not supported yet, send us your roster from inside the app (a one-tap consented flow) or email it to support@altivra.io — we'll work on it. Manual entry and CSV import work for every pilot in the meantime.
Is it EFB-approved?
Altivra is not a certified EFB — it's a personal productivity app for pilots. It lives alongside your company EFB and handles the parts those apps don't touch: your personal logbook, rest, pay, and career data. Some airlines allow Altivra on the same iPad as your EFB; check with your operator.
Can I import my existing logbook?
Yes. Logbook migration from any major pilot logbook app or any standard CSV/spreadsheet format. The importer handles common aircraft type codes and airport codes automatically, and flags anything ambiguous for you to confirm. Bring your full career — every hour, every airport, every aircraft.
Do I need an Apple Watch for the fatigue features?

No. Altivra reads sleep data from Apple HealthKit, which aggregates input from any source you already use — Apple Watch, ring trackers, fitness wearables, or manual entry in the Health app. Your sleep data flows into HealthKit and then into Altivra automatically, regardless of which tracker you use.

A dedicated Altivra Apple Watch app (notifications, on-wrist quick checks) arrives in v1.1. The iPhone companion ships at launch.

Who is Altivra built for?
Every pilot who logs flights. Commercial airline pilots are the core — the roster import, payslip dispute workflow, and crew memory features were built around the airline workflow. But the logbook, fatigue tracking, calculators, EFB tools, and airport intelligence fit any pilot: regional, corporate, GA, helicopter, military. If you fly and you log it, Altivra was built for you.
Can I pay monthly?
Yes — $99/year or $9.99/month. Same features either way. Billed through the App Store.
What is Wingman?

Wingman answers questions about your own flying — your logbook, roster and rest — and looks up FAR 117 / EASA / AFAC regulations, instantly and offline. Ask "how many layovers in Tokyo this quarter?" or "what's my 28-day flight time?" and get the answer from your own data.

Conversational AI powered by a language model arrives in a future update — until then, Wingman is a fast, offline lookup, not a chatbot.

What about FlightAware — is there an extra cost?

No. FlightAware enrichment is included with Altivra at no extra cost. Each logged flight is enriched once (real routing, gates, actual block times) and saved — re-checks only when you ask for one.

A fair-use quota of 150 lookups per month comfortably covers a full airline month of flying. No add-ons, no bundles, no separate key required.

How does FlightAware enrichment work?
Every flight you log is automatically matched against FlightAware's flight data — actual routing, gate and terminal assignments, real block times, diversions. You don't enter anything beyond the flight number and date. The enrichment runs server-side once per flight, so your iPad isn't making API calls in flight. Included with Altivra, fair-use quota.
